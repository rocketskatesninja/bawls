Getting started with The Ball™

From box to first roll in under a minute. Most customers manage it in one.

Updated Sep 22, 2026 · 1 min read

Unbox it

Open the box and remove The Ball™. Keep the box: it makes an excellent stand.

Your first roll

  1. Place The Ball™ on a flat, horizontal surface.
  2. Apply gentle force in the direction you’d like it to go.
  3. Observe. That’s rolling.

Don’t put The Ball™ on a slope until you’ve completed your first roll. It knows what to do on a slope. You may not.

Set up BallOps™

Your welcome email has a link to sign in to BallOps™, where you register your sphere, track roundness and download compliance certificates. No email? Ask your administrator, then see trouble signing in.

Invite your team

On Professional and Enterprise, administrators invite people from Admin › People › Invite, and each person takes a seat. Starter is for one person. Using single sign-on? Skip the invitations and provision with SCIM.

Care and storage

  • Store at room temperature, away from stairs.
  • Clean with a soft, dry cloth. Do not iron.
  • The Ball™ never needs charging, updating or a firmware password.
