Product FAQ

Questions, answered

What’s the difference between the three balls?

The Ball™ and The Ball™ Pro are the same ball. Pro adds BallOps™ features for teams: single sign-on, an SLA and a success manager. The Ball™ Lite is a drawing of the ball.

Can I try it before I buy?

Yes. The Ball™ Lite is free forever. Print it, evaluate it, and upgrade when you’re ready to roll.

Do I need BallOps™?

No. The Ball™ works without software. BallOps™ is for teams who need to manage, audit or simply admire their spheres.

Is there a warranty?

Every ball carries a lifetime roundness warranty: if it ever stops being round, we replace it. How to check roundness.

Do you ship internationally?

We ship to 140 countries from Sphereville, Dublin and Singapore. Spheres travel well.

Can I get a custom color?

Custom colorways come with Enterprise. Talk to sales.
