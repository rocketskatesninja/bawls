Trouble signing in

Most sign-in problems take under two minutes to fix. Work through these in order.

Updated Nov 14, 2026 · 2 min read

Never share your password. Bawls Inc. will never ask for it by email, phone, chat or carrier pigeon. If someone does, they are not us.

Make sure you’re in the right place

BallOps™ lives at /portal. Bookmarks from the old Sphere Manager (retired in 2019) no longer work, and neither does Sphere Manager.

“Incorrect email or password”

  • Use your work email: the one your administrator invited. A personal address is not an account, however round you are.
  • Passwords are case-sensitive, so check Caps Lock. The ball is not case-sensitive.
  • If your company uses single sign-on, you don’t have a BallOps™ password. Open BallOps™ from your identity provider’s app launcher instead. See single sign-on.
  • Still no luck? Reset your password.

Your account is locked

After five unsuccessful attempts, BallOps™ locks the account for 15 minutes to keep it safe. Every attempt during the lock restarts the timer, so the fastest fix is to stop trying. Step away. Roll something.

Locked again as soon as the timer ends? Your administrator can unlock you from Admin › People, or reset your password to clear the lock.

Your two-step code isn’t accepted

Codes change every 30 seconds and rely on your phone’s clock. Turn on automatic date and time, wait for a fresh code, and try once. Lost the device? See lost your authenticator.

Your browser is the problem

  • Signing in needs cookies and JavaScript. Allow both for this site.
  • Clear the cache, or try a private window.
  • We support the last two versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge. Internet Explorer is not supported, and has not been round for some time.

It might be us

Check system status. If Sign-in & SSO shows anything other than Operational, we already know, and the ball is being re-inflated.

Still can’t sign in?

Open a ticket with your work email, the time you tried and the exact message you saw. Leave your password out: we never need it.
