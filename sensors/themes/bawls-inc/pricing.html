Pricing FAQ

Before you buy

Is there a free trial?

The Ball™ Lite is free forever. Professional also comes with a 30-day roll-back guarantee: if it isn’t round enough, we refund you.

What counts as a seat?

Anyone who signs in to BallOps™. The ball itself doesn’t need a seat; it prefers to stand.

Can I change plans later?

Upgrade any time and pay the difference; downgrades apply at renewal. How plan changes work.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major cards. Enterprise can pay by invoice against a purchase order.

Do prices include tax?

No. Sales tax or VAT is added at checkout, based on your billing address.

What happens if I cancel?

Your plan runs to the end of the term, and you keep the ball. Cancelling a plan.
