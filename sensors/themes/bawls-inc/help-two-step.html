Two-step verification

A second check at sign-in, so a stolen password isn’t enough on its own.

Updated Oct 30, 2026 · 1 min read

Turn it on

  1. Sign in to BallOps™ and open Profile › Security.
  2. Choose Set up two-step verification.
  3. Scan the code with Roundness Authenticator™ or any authenticator app (TOTP).
  4. Enter the six-digit code to confirm, then save your backup codes somewhere safe.

Administrators on Professional and Enterprise can require two-step verification for everyone from Admin › Security.

Codes aren’t accepted

Codes depend on the time on your phone. Switch on automatic date and time, wait for a new code, and enter it once. Wrong codes count toward the sign-in lock.

Lost your authenticator

  • Use one of your backup codes in place of the six-digit code. Each one works once.
  • No backup codes? Your administrator can reset two-step verification for you from Admin › People.
  • If you are the only administrator, contact support. We confirm who you are before we change anything, which takes one to three rolling days.

Text messages

We don’t send codes by text message. Texts can be intercepted, and the ball does not trust them.
