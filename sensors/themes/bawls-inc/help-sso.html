Single sign-on (SAML & OIDC)

Let your team sign in with the identity provider they already use. Included with Professional and Enterprise.

Updated Nov 3, 2026 · 1 min read

Supported providers

Any SAML 2.0 or OpenID Connect provider works. We have step-by-step guides for Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, OneLogin, JumpCloud and the Sphere Identity Cloud.

Set up SAML

  1. In BallOps™, open Admin › Security › Single sign-on and choose SAML 2.0.
  2. Copy the Entity ID and ACS URL shown there into a new app in your identity provider.
  3. Send the email address as the NameID, in email format.
  4. Paste your provider’s metadata URL into BallOps™ and choose Test connection.
  5. When the test passes, turn on Require SSO. Keep one administrator on password sign-in as a break-glass account.

Set up OpenID Connect

Choose OpenID Connect instead, add the redirect URI shown to your provider, then paste the client ID, client secret and issuer URL into BallOps™. It needs the scopes openid email profile.

Provision users with SCIM

Professional and Enterprise can create and remove people automatically with SCIM 2.0.

Generate a SCIM token under Admin › Security. It is shown once, so store it in your identity provider straight away.

Common errors

  • “User not assigned”: add the person to the BallOps™ app in your identity provider.
  • “Invalid audience”: the Entity ID in your provider doesn’t match the one in BallOps™. Copy it again; it is case-sensitive.
  • Signed in, then signed straight out: your provider’s clock is more than five minutes off.
