Company

Nearly thirty years of round.

Bawls Inc. was founded on a single, radical premise: that the world needed a better ball.

Our mission

To make the world more round, one sphere at a time.

We believe roundness is not a feature — it is a responsibility. Since 1997 we have shipped a single product and refined it relentlessly. We do not make cubes. We do not make cylinders. We make the ball.

Our history
  • 1997 — Bawls Inc. founded in a garage in Sphereville. The first prototype is slightly oval.
  • 2004 — Achieves 99% roundness. Opens the Rolling Plaza headquarters.
  • 2011 — Ships the millionth ball. It is the same ball.
  • 2019 — Surpasses 4,000 enterprise customers.
  • 2025 — Closes a Series B and publishes the first Roundness Report.
  • 2026 — Launches The Ball™ Pro. It is also the same ball.

Our values

What we believe

Roundness first

Every decision starts with one question: is it round? If not, we don’t ship it.

Do one thing

We make one product. We have been asked to make a cube. We said no.

Customer-obsessed

Our customers tell us what they need. It is almost always a ball.

Radical transparency

You can see the whole ball from here. We have nothing to hide, and nowhere to hide it.

Built to last

A ball from 1997 still rolls today. We measure product life in decades.

Bias for rolling

When in doubt, we roll. Momentum is a company value and a law of physics.
