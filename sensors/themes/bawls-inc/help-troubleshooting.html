When The Ball™ won’t roll

Rolling problems, roundness concerns, and what to do when it gets away from you.

Updated Aug 8, 2026 · 1 min read

It won’t roll

  1. Check the surface is horizontal. Walls are not supported.
  2. Check it is The Ball™ and not a photo of The Ball™.
  3. Apply gentle force. If that fails, apply slightly less gentle force.

It rolled away

This is expected behavior. Check under the sofa, then the lowest point in the room. Enterprise customers with the rolling SLA can request a retrieval specialist.

It doesn’t seem round

BallOps™ measures roundness under Spheres › Roundness, and anything above 99.7% is within spec. Below that, open a ticket and we’ll send a replacement. Yours may simply be tired.

It makes a noise

A faint rolling sound is normal. Squeaking, humming or speech is not, and should be reported to us and possibly to someone else.

Factory reset

Pick up The Ball™. Put it down. The reset is complete.
