Sign-in FAQ

Quick answers

I forgot my password.

Enter your work email on the reset page and we’ll send a link. It lasts 30 minutes.

Why is my account locked?

Five unsuccessful attempts lock an account for 15 minutes, and each new attempt restarts the timer. Wait it out. More about locks.

The reset email never arrived.

Check spam or quarantine, and request only once: each request cancels the last link. More help.

My company uses single sign-on. Which password do I use?

Your company’s. Open BallOps™ from your identity provider, and reset the password there. About single sign-on.

I lost the phone with my authenticator.

Use a backup code, or ask your administrator to reset two-step verification. Step by step.

Can support tell me my password?

No. We can’t see it, and we will never ask for it. Anyone who asks is not us.
