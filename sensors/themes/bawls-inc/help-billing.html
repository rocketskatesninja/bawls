Billing and plans

Plans, seats, invoices, and how to change any of them.

Updated Oct 12, 2026 · 1 min read

Billing is for administrators. Can’t get in? See trouble signing in.

Plans

Starter is a one-time purchase for one person. Professional is billed per seat, and Enterprise is priced for your deployment. Every plan includes the same ball. See pricing for what changes.

Invoices and receipts

Administrators can download every invoice from BallOps™ › Admin › Billing. Need a tax ID or purchase order number on it? Add it there before your next renewal: we can’t change an invoice once it’s issued.

Change plan or seats

  • Upgrades take effect straight away, and you pay for the rest of the term.
  • Downgrades and seat reductions apply at renewal.
  • Moving to Enterprise? Talk to sales about annual terms and a dedicated sphere-success manager.

A payment failed

We retry a declined card three times over a week and email your billing contact each time.

Update the card under Admin › Billing to stop the retries. Your account stays active for 14 days after a failed payment.

Cancel your plan

Cancel any time from Admin › Billing; your plan runs to the end of the term. The Ball™ is yours to keep. It was always yours.
