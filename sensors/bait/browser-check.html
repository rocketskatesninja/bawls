bawls · see what your browser reveals

⚠ Read this before you run it

This page actively fingerprints your browser. When you press the button it probes your canvas, WebGL, audio stack, installed fonts, screen, hardware, network and more to build an identifier that can recognise your browser across sites — then it shows you the result and records it for this site's operator.

It runs nothing until you choose to. If you don't want your fingerprint computed or recorded, just close this page.

Note: your ad/tracker blocker almost certainly won't stop this. Blockers work from lists of known third-party tracker domains; this script is first-party and inline, so it isn't on any list. That's the whole point of the test — it shows what first-party fingerprinting can see regardless of your blocker.
